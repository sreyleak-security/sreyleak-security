# Hi, I'm Srey Leak 👋

**Independent security researcher & anti-scam educator** based in Long Beach, California.
Son of Cambodian refugees. Previously: **Chainalysis** (blockchain forensics, 2016–2019) and **Coinbase** (security engineering, 2019–2022).

After the June 2026 Polymarket frontend hack ($3.1M across 11 accounts — Polymarket refunded every victim itself), I started publishing what I learned: how scams actually work, and how to make sure you're never the next one.

> **Prevention, not recovery.** If someone asks for your seed phrase, it's a scam. Period. Even if they speak your language.
