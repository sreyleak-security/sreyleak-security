### Hi, I'm Srey Leak 👋

Security researcher & anti-scam educator based in **Long Beach, California**.
Son of Cambodian refugees. Previously **Chainalysis** (blockchain forensics,
2016–2019) and **Coinbase** (security engineering, 2019–2022).

After the **June 2026 Polymarket frontend hack** ($3.1M across 11 accounts —
Polymarket itself refunded every victim), I started publishing what I learned:
how scams actually work, and how to not be the next one.

**Prevention, not recovery.** If someone asks for your seed phrase, it's a scam. Period.
